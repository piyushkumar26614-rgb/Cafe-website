Our Story

More Than
Just Coffee

Nestled inside The Elite City Center on Model Town Road, Clubhouse Cafe was born from a simple dream — to create a space where everyone feels at home. Whether you're here for a quiet morning espresso, a working lunch, or a long evening with friends, we've got the perfect corner for you.

We take pride in being an LGBTQ+ friendly and women-owned establishment. Our menu celebrates fresh, flavourful ingredients with generous vegetarian options — because great food should be for everyone.
